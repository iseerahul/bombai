Bambai — landing page brief

One job: get a thumb onto Explore Bambai, which opens the map at /app/. Everything else is persuasion.

Existing visual language — keep it. Bebas Neue display caps, DM Mono for eyebrow labels, Manrope for body. Pale-blue --accent and cream --background alternating by section, near-black --foreground, vermilion --primary for CTAs and one accent word. Thick black borders, hard offset drop-shadows, tilted framed images, sticker cutouts. Editorial collage, not a SaaS page.

1 · Hero — the city, from above

Full-viewport. The Mumbai map is the background, not a screenshot in a frame — the illustrated coastline from the northern suburbs down to Colaba, washed back so type sits over it. Neighbourhood pins float on it (Bandra · 12 plans, Juhu · sunset crew, Worli · 7 events, Colaba · art walk), drifting gently so the city reads as live.

Sticker cutouts break the frame at the edges: auto-rickshaw, cutting chai, vada pav, two friends mid-laugh. Rotated a few degrees, overlapping the map.

Eyebrow, mono caps: MUMBAI AFTER HOURS

Headline, huge condensed caps: BAMBAI SIDE UP

One line under it: Find the event. Meet the crew. Make the city yours.

EXPLORE BAMBAI — vermilion pill, black border, hard shadow. The only loud button on the screen.

Live ticker at the foot: 42 plans live · 2.4K city people

Cream background. Four cards, staggered tilts, each numbered 01–04 with a Devanagari kicker.
