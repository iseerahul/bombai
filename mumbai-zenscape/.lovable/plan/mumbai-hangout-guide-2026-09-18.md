# Mumbai Hangout Guide

## What I’ll build
- Replace the blank screen with a polished one-page Mumbai hangout guide for Gen Z.
- Use a bright white foundation with restrained glass panels, bold editorial typography, playful cutout layering, and lively color accents.
- Feature the third and fourth uploaded artworks directly as major visual moments; use the first two only to guide the collage style.
- Add original cutout-style visuals of Mumbai landmarks including Bandra–Worli Sea Link, Gateway of India, and the Taj Mahal Palace.
- Organize the page around quick discovery: featured hangouts, neighborhood picks, mood filters, and a compact city guide/footer.
- Make navigation, filters, save controls, and featured-place actions interactive without adding accounts or stored data.

## Visual direction
- Editorial street-culture collage rather than a conventional tourism page.
- Crisp white space, translucent panels, sharp typography, sticker-like landmarks, hand-drawn accents, and controlled overlaps.
- Mobile and desktop layouts will preserve readable text and strong first-screen impact.

## Technical details
- Build the page in the existing TanStack React app and define all styling through semantic design tokens.
- Store the two uploaded artworks through the project’s asset system.
- Generate transparent landmark cutouts for the page rather than using stock placeholders.
- Add page-specific title, description, and social metadata.
- Verify the finished page visually at desktop and mobile sizes and confirm the preview builds cleanly.
